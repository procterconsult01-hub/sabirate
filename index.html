Why the same $500 pays out differently everywhere

Rate

Every provider sets its own naira exchange rate, usually a little below the real mid-market rate. That markup is where most of them quietly make their money — not the fee.

Fee

Some charge a flat fee, some a percentage, some both. A $0 fee with a bad rate can cost you more than a $4 fee with a good one.

Speed & payout method

Bank transfer, mobile wallet, or cash pickup — faster and more convenient options sometimes carry the worst rates. Worth checking before you assume "instant" is free.

This is a comparison tool, not a money transmitter. SabiRate does not move funds, hold money, or process transfers. It shows published rate and fee data so you can decide which provider to use directly.

The mid-market exchange rate shown is live, pulled from a public FX data source on page load. Provider markups and fees are checked periodically, not in real time — before sending, confirm the current price on the provider's own site or app.
